# Demo camera permission

The application does not ship a camera catalog. This template is for footage or
screenshots used in the project's README, website, release notes, or other demos.

Send the request to the camera operator and retain their written response in the
private project records. Do not commit personal contact information to the repo.

## Request template

> Hello,
>
> I maintain `monitorthesituation`, a free, MIT-licensed terminal application
> that converts live video into ASCII/Unicode artwork locally on a user's
> computer. It does not record or redistribute streams.
>
> May we connect to your camera for a project demonstration and publish short
> screen recordings or screenshots of its transformed ASCII output?
>
> We request permission to:
>
> 1. Access the live stream for local playback and transformation.
> 2. Display transformed ASCII/Unicode output in project demonstrations.
> 3. Publish screenshots or short recordings of that transformed output.
> 4. Identify and link to your organization as the camera operator.
>
> The stream URL will not be bundled into the application or public catalog. We
> will use this attribution unless you prefer another: “[Camera name], courtesy
> of [operator], transformed locally by monitorthesituation.”
>
> Please also tell us about any time limit, logo, link, geographic, commercial,
> or revocation conditions you require.

## Demo checklist

- [ ] The responder controls or is authorized to license the camera.
- [ ] Local frame transformation is explicitly permitted.
- [ ] Publishing screenshots or recordings is explicitly permitted.
- [ ] Required attribution is documented verbatim.
- [ ] Platform terms also permit the technical access method.
- [ ] No private areas or unexpectedly identifiable subjects are emphasized.
- [ ] Permission and operator contact have been reverified in the past year.
